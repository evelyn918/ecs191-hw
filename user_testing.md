The patterns you saw across users. Where did multiple people get confused?
What you would change about your app based on what you learned?

Most users were able to complete the survey and correctly tell me what the app was -- an app that helps you prepare what to bring based on the forecast. Multiple people got confused on where to proceed after completing the survery at the begining of launching the app due to the "Done" button not being very visible in the top right corner, especially in night mode. To proceed with the scenario and AI result portion of the interview, I did point out the button to get users who were confused to the main screen to proceed with the interview, but refrained from any other guideance. I also saw that not many people were tapping on the screen much (selecting different timeframes, settings, switching from hourly forecast to 7-day forecast). This tells me that the buttons were not very accessible or noticable to users. As such, I would change the button visibility for my app to make it easier to notice and use or even consider removing functionality such as the setting for the day (selecting: class/work, outdoor, evening out).
